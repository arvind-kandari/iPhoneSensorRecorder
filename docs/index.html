Getting started

Basic usage

  1. 01

    Allow access

    Grant camera and motion access when prompted. Grant microphone access if you plan to record audio.

  2. 02

    Choose camera settings

    Select a supported resolution and frame rate, then frame your shot in the live preview.

  3. 03

    Record

    Tap Record. Use Pause and Resume within the current recording, or Stop to finish it.

  4. 04

    Transfer your files

    Open Recordings to review locally stored sessions and transfer the files you need.

What is recorded

One session, coordinated outputs

Camera video

The selected iPhone camera records video to video.mov.

Accelerometer

Measures acceleration along the device's X, Y, and Z axes.

Gyroscope

Measures rotation rate around the device's three axes.

Magnetometer

Records magnetic-field measurements reported by the device.

Device attitude

Records calculated attitude and orientation values.

Sensor timestamps

Timing information relates sensor measurements to the recording timeline.

Audio

Microphone audio is included when audio recording is enabled and permission is granted.

CSV and metadata

Measurements are saved in sensors.csv and session details in metadata.json.

Your recordings

Stored locally on your iPhone

Each completed recording folder contains its video, sensor CSV, and metadata file. Recordings remain local until you transfer them using available iOS file-sharing options.

Troubleshooting

Common checks

The camera preview is unavailable

In iPhone Settings, confirm camera permission is enabled for CapturE, then reopen the app.

Sensor data is missing

Confirm Motion & Fitness access is allowed, then start a new recording.

Audio is missing

Check that audio recording was enabled and microphone permission was granted before recording.

A resolution or frame rate is not listed

CapturE shows combinations supported by the selected camera. Choices vary by camera and iPhone model.

I cannot find a recording

Check Recordings and the CapturE location exposed through iOS Files or device file sharing.
